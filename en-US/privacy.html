Additives: E-Number Decoder

Privacy Policy

Additives collects no personal data, creates no accounts, includes no analytics or ads, and runs offline on your device.

Help Privacy Policy All Languages

Data Handling Summary

Camera & PhotosProcessed entirely on-device to recognize ingredient text. Images are never uploaded or saved to disk or cloud.
Watch List & Saved ReadingsStored locally inside the app container on your device (saved.json and UserDefaults). No account or cloud sync.
Analytics, Ads & TrackingNone. Zero third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs are included.
Network RequestsApple StoreKit 2 only, used solely when fetching pricing, purchasing, or restoring Additives Pro.

No Personal Data Collection

Additives does not collect, store on external servers, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, identifiers, location data, or usage telemetry.

Camera & Photo Library Access

Camera and photo access are optional and requested only when you tap Read or Choose a Photo. You can also type or paste text without granting media access. Selected photos and live video frames are analyzed in memory to extract additive names and E-numbers and are immediately discarded.

Local Storage & Deletion

Your saved readings, favourites, watch list selections, and preferences stay solely on your iPhone or iPad (using UserDefaults for preference flags and the verified StoreKit unlock cache, plus an app sandbox JSON file). You can erase saved history anytime from Settings, or remove the app to delete all local data.

In-App Purchase

Additives Pro is a single non-consumable purchase processed directly by Apple via StoreKit 2. We never receive payment details or your Apple Account information.
