How Additives Works

  1. Read an ingredient list — Open the Read tab and point the camera at the ingredients line on a food package. Codes such as E330, E 129, or e120 and additive names are outlined live on the image. Tap Read this label for a full breakdown, or pick a photo or type/paste text.
  2. Highlight what you watch first — Open Watch For (Shelf or Read) to select up to three single E-numbers for free, or entire additive groups with Additives Pro. Watched items appear at the top of every report with a yellow marker.
  3. Review EU labelling notices — Beneath your watch list, the report lists statutory package statements such as sulphite allergen emphasis (10 mg/kg SO2), the six children's activity/attention colours (Reg. (EC) No 1333/2008 Art. 24 & Annex V), aspartame phenylalanine notice, >10% polyols notice, and codes no longer on the EU food additive list (e.g. E171 titanium dioxide, E128 Red 2G).
  4. Browse 339 EU E-numbers offline — Use Look Up to browse by number range or functional class, search by code or name in 12 languages, and check EU status and EU-specified origins (such as insects, egg, or milk sugar). No scores or medical advice are shown.
  5. Additives Pro (one-time purchase) — Unlocks whole Watch For groups and unlimited single E-numbers, saving named readings on your device, side-by-side label comparison, favourites, and plain-text export.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Additives upload package photos?

No. Camera frames and selected photos are processed exclusively on-device using Apple Vision text recognition and immediately discarded.

Does it give health scores or medical advice?

No. Additives is an offline reference decoder built from the consolidated EU food additive register (Reg. (EC) No 1333/2008 Annex II, consolidated 18 August 2026) and EU labelling law. It presents factual entries so you can decide.

Can it read foreign packaging?

Yes. It detects which of its 12 supported languages an ingredient list is printed in, matches names in that language, and shows results in your app language — including INS numbers printed without 'E' and US dye names like Red 40.

How do I restore Additives Pro?

Open Settings (top left on Read, Look Up, or Shelf) and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
